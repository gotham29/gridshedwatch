Storms together

All grids shift as one, because events in one storm aren’t independent.

2,000 shifts; p = (1 + shifts catching at least as many) / 2,001; Holm-corrected across the 11 methods

Each draw shifts every grid’s alerts by the same whole number of years (1 to 5) plus up to ±15 days, wrapped within the first 6 whole years of data. Why shared across grids: 7 of the 8 load sheds come from two storms; shifting each grid separately would treat them as independent and make p-values too small. Smallest possible p with 2,000 draws is 0.0005; after Holm over 11 methods, 0.0055. This test controls for each method’s own alert rate, which is why it, not raw counts, decides “carries warning”. If asked about a cluster bootstrap by storm: with only 3 storms among the Tier 1 events it is meaningless; we report leave-one-storm-out results instead (appendix). Intervals on catch rates are exact Clopper–Pearson.
