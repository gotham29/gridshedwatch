Data

48 grids of public hourly data, frozen, and five signals every method sees

48 grids

Every EIA-930 balancing authority with 90% coverage. 9 had an event; 39 are quiet.

Frozen

Demand, day-ahead forecast and net generation, 2019–2026, with checksums, because EIA revises past values.

5 signals

How far demand misses the operator’s own forecast, how much power the grid imports, and how unusual each is for the time of year.

2019 is for calibration only; scoring starts in January 2020. No weather, outage or price data: the benchmark asks what public grid totals alone can warn of.

Code MIT, data and results CC BY 4.0, Croissant metadata and datasheet included

Coverage rule: at least 90% of hours with demand, forecast and net generation in 2019 and in the scored period, after a bad-data filter set on 2019 (values at or below zero, or above twice the 2019 99.9th percentile). Signals: forecast error = demand minus day-ahead forecast; imbalance = demand minus net generation; seasonal anomaly = value minus the median of the same hour 1 to 4 weeks earlier. Data-quality flags (reported, never dropped): flat demand for 48 h or more (SWPP, LDWP, IID); a change in how the forecast is reported (CHPD, WACM). Known limit: hourly totals for the whole BA, so a shortfall in part of a large BA (MISO South within MISO) is diluted.
