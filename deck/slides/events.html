Complete under the rule; checked against NERC’s load-shed tables and DOE OE-417. Two EEA 3 emergencies without a shed are reported as case studies

The rule, fixed in advance: an operator-ordered firm load shed of at least 250 MW because supply was insufficient, in a balancing authority reporting to EIA-930, Jan 2020 to Sept 2026. Onset = the operator’s emergency declaration; where unpublished (Duke Carolinas, Duke Progress) the first load-shed directive. Exclusions (all logged in docs/events.md): El Paso Electric 218 MW and Imperial Irrigation District 100 MW (under the floor), Hurricane Laura and wildfire shutoffs (not supply), the Columbus derecho (transmission), a 2022 CAISO-filed shed in Baja California, MISO’s May 2025 New Orleans shed (transmission emergency), Dominion SC and Santee Cooper during Elliott (under the floor). Onsets checked against OE-417 start times; a sensitivity run with the earlier of the two costs the four leading methods at most 1 watch each (Chronos-Bolt loses 2), because the earlier OE-417 starts for SPP, TVA and Duke Carolinas cover the whole incident. Development events (used to design the alert rules): ERCOT, CAISO 2020, TVA; the other five are held out. Tier 2 case studies: NV Energy Aug 2020, CAISO Sept 2022 (strict EEA 3, no shed, two sources).
