Why event scoring

Scored hour by hour, random scores come first

Point-adjusted F1, still common in anomaly detection, ranks random scores above every method on the same grids and events. An hourly score can’t see what an operator needs: one alert in time, and few elsewhere.

Per-hour metrics computed with TSB-AD’s code on the 9 grids with events, under two labelings

Labelings: the 48 hours from each onset, and the 168 hours before it. PA-F1 ranks random scores first under both (0.088 and 0.364, against at most 0.054 and 0.242 for any method). VUS-PR, TSB-AD’s recommended metric, agrees only weakly with events caught.
